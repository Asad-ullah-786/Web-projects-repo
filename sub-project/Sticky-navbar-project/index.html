Wellcome to our website

After his first adventure with Bulma and the others, Goku’s passion for martial arts burned even brighter.
He had already shown incredible talent for fighting, but he knew there was much more to learn. Determined to improve, he sought out Master Roshi, the wise and eccentric Turtle Hermit, whose skills were legendary. Roshi was not the type to accept any student easily; he wanted to see determination, discipline, and spirit. As part of his unusual test, Roshi asked Goku to bring him a beautiful young woman so that he might consider taking him as a disciple. In the process, Goku met Krillin, a short but ambitious fighter from a distant temple who also sought Roshi’s training. The two quickly became rivals, each trying to impress Roshi enough to be accepted. Roshi eventually agreed to train both of them, but his lessons were unlike anything they had expected. Instead of endless combat drills, the training involved strange tasks like delivering milk across rough terrain, plowing fields without machines, and wearing heavy turtle shells while performing everyday chores.
At first, it seemed like meaningless labor, but Goku’s natural enthusiasm and boundless energy helped him excel. Over time, he and Krillin developed incredible strength, stamina, and agility. Goku especially thrived in this environment, treating even the most exhausting work as a game. His cheerful nature made him push past his limits without complaint. Months later, Master Roshi decided his students were ready for their first true test — the World Martial Arts Tournament. This was a prestigious competition where the strongest fighters from around the globe gathered to prove their skill. Goku and Krillin entered the tournament under the names of Roshi’s school, but Roshi himself also joined, disguised as “Jackie Chun” to test his students in secret.
The battles were intense, and Goku’s raw talent and unshakable will quickly caught everyone’s attention. He faced strong opponents, learned new techniques, and discovered that martial arts was as much about strategy as it was about strength. In the finals, Goku found himself up against Jackie Chun, not realizing it was his own master. The fight was fierce, with Goku pushing himself further than ever before. He used speed, creativity, and sheer determination to match Chun blow for blow. At one point, the fight continued into the night, and Goku accidentally looked at the full moon. In an instant, he transformed into the massive Great Ape once again.
The crowd panicked, and Jackie Chun was forced to destroy the moon to stop Goku’s rampage. When Goku returned to normal, the fight resumed, but in the end, Jackie Chun narrowly defeated him. Despite losing, Goku was far from discouraged. He saw the loss as motivation to train even harder, determined that one day he would win. After the tournament, Goku set out on a new journey to recover the Dragon Balls that had been scattered once again.
His travels took him across dangerous mountains, deserts, and enemy hideouts. He faced powerful foes like the Red Ribbon Army, a ruthless organization seeking the Dragon Balls for world domination. Against overwhelming numbers, Goku fought fearlessly, dismantling their forces piece by piece. Along the way, he made new allies such as Android 8, a gentle giant with a kind heart, and strengthened his bonds with old friends. His journey against the Red Ribbon Army became one of the first true demonstrations of his ability to overcome impossible odds through sheer courage and willpower.
